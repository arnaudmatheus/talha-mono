FONTLOG for Talha Mono
======================

Basic font information
----------------------
Talha Mono is a monospaced font for terminals and desktop interfaces, made for
the Jandí theme for Omarchy. It is a derivative of Monaspace Radon: Radon's
cursive letters are redrawn with a simulated Renaissance broad-nib pen held at
one fixed angle, while Radon's vertical metrics, symbols, box drawing and
OpenType features are kept. Braille is only in the NF builds (upstream Radon
has none).

Six families are built from the same sources:
- Talha Mono         Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic (cell 1160)
- Talha Mono NF      the same, plus Nerd Fonts icons
- Talha Mono Wide    the same letters in a 1320 cell, for extra-wide spacing
- Talha Mono Wide NF the same, plus Nerd Fonts icons
- Talha Mono Text    Regular and Italic, nib 8% wider (weight 450), for
                     light backgrounds and low-density screens
- Talha Mono Text NF the same, plus Nerd Fonts icons

Released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt). Following the
OFL, it does not use the Reserved Font Names of the original ("Monaspace",
"Argon", "Neon", "Xenon", "Radon", "Krypton").

ChangeLog
---------
1.0.0-beta.7 (2026-10-24): t, l and 1 refined; contextual spacing
The first published release since 1.0.0-beta.3. It includes the local
beta.4, beta.5 and beta.6 candidates listed below.
- t, l and 1 redrawn slightly: the t bar sits 20 units lower, the l tail
  reaches up to 20 units further right at the baseline, and the 1 flag is
  10 units longer. Accented forms, lslash, texture-healing and SS11 forms,
  hints and the NF copies follow. Chosen by the user on 2026-10-24; the
  recorded blind ratings so far compare spacing only. The cv10 l/lslash,
  cv02 one and the fraction figures keep their upstream drawings.
- Optional ss11, Contextual spacing (beta.5, guarded in beta.6), now with a
  pruned lookup: Regular is 18% smaller and its GSUB 36% smaller, with the
  same shaping in 447,600 compared runs. Off by default; advances never change.
- Accent and mark-attachment fixes from beta.5: stacked and below marks,
  NFC/NFD equivalence, accented healing forms drawn from Talha's own recipes.
- From beta.4: balanced roman i, letter-specific swsh A/P/R, scalloped
  leaves and folded ribbon ends, and Nerd Font icon names made PostScript-safe.
- The legibility gate against beta.3 passes in all ten styles with no waiver;
  the most confusable pair improves in every style. Contextual checks pass in
  all 20 faces. See docs/design/beta7.md.

1.0.0-beta.6 (2026-10-23): local spacing refinement
- Guard optional ss11 shifts with shared-height contour gaps. Keep fixed
  cells, 40-unit ink shifts, and all existing letterform drawings.
- Group accents with their base bodies; compile guards as class-based
  contextual extension subtables, preserving existing OpenType features.
- Add all-style and separate-corpus comparisons, feature-off pixel checks,
  advance/overlap checks and a three-column native Pango proof.
- See docs/design/beta6.md for results, shaping cost and remaining tradeoffs.

1.0.0-beta.5 (2026-10-22): local contextual-feature candidate
- Regenerate accented healing forms from Talha's own pen recipes, including
  dotless j and extended Latin precompositions. Attach combining marks to
  every supported default and contextual base, with GDEF base classes and
  transformed GPOS anchors. The beta.3/beta.4 stacked-mark failures are fixed.
- Add opt-in ss11, Contextual spacing: position the middle letter using both
  neighbors' optical body widths. All advances remain fixed. Existing GSUB,
  texture healing and ligatures are preserved; ss11 is independent of liga.
- Whole-run HarfBuzz/FreeType measurements at native reading sizes, 20/40/60
  unit spacing previews, Pango proofs and anonymous personal reading samples.
- Add a contextual shaping/advance audit to CI. Rebuild all 20 faces; default
  legibility and full-family FontBakery checks pass. See docs/design/beta5.md.

1.0.0-beta.4 (2026-10-21): local candidate
- Balanced roman i entries and exits, inherited by dotless i, accented forms,
  the Turkish local form and texture-healing alternates. Character advances
  stay fixed. The corsiva keeps its distinct i.
- Letter-specific swsh capitals A, P and R: a crossbar loop, a descending
  stem flourish, and a running leg. Three studies each; the middle treatment
  is selected in the source build.
- A scalloped scroll leaf with ribs, in eight directional/turned forms,
  and folded ribbon ends with a rear swallowtail and crease. Existing
  Unicode mappings and display feature tags are retained.
- Source atlas, comparison sheets, 48/96/192 px display proofs, native
  12/15/20 px reading proofs, and composed title/chapter pages in docs/design/.
- Normalize Nerd Font icon names to PostScript-safe names, handling collisions
  and aliases without changing Unicode coverage, icon outlines or advances.
- The regression gate now checks all ten styles independently, including
  Bold Italic and the Wide/Text styles previously covered only in comparisons.
- Local builds can use HINT_JOBS=1 when sandbox restrictions prevent the
  autohinter's parallel workers. The default remains two workers per font.
- All 20 faces are rebuilt under build/. The checked-in fonts/otf/ remain
  the beta.3 baseline. Candidate notes and validation: docs/design/beta4.md.

1.0.0-beta.3 (2026-10-19): renamed Talha Mono
- The font was Jandí Mono until 1.0.0-beta.2. It is not affiliated with
  jandi-colors (whose palette and name the Jandí theme borrows) nor with
  the JANDI messenger, so it takes its own name: talha, the gilded
  carving of the churches of Minas Gerais, where its ornament comes from.
- Families: Talha Mono, Talha Mono NF, Talha Mono Wide (and NF), Talha
  Mono Text (and NF); files TalhaMono-*.otf. The glyphs are the same as
  1.0.0-beta.2.

1.0.0-beta.2 (2026-10-18): t and r told apart
- The roman t's bar crosses its stem (330-800, was 430-880), the shape of
  the t in JetBrains Mono and Iosevka, the fonts with the most distinct t/r
  of eleven measured. t/r in Jandi Mono: 0.936 -> 0.916 at 12 px, 0.911 ->
  0.889 at 15 px, 0.901 -> 0.849 at 20 px; the same in Bold, Wide and Text.
- The 1's flag starts further out, so 1 stays apart from the new t; 1/l
  improves too (0.888 -> 0.816 at 20 px). The foot stays: without it, 1
  read as the new t.
- The cost, accepted on purpose: t/1 (0.736 -> 0.840 at 20 px, from the
  font's most distinct pair) and t/f (about +0.02). The most confusable
  pair involving t still improves at every size. The regression gate now
  takes waivers scoped to one version (tools/gate_waivers.json), and lists
  each accepted regression in its report.
- The Italics keep their t: against the corsiva's short-armed r the
  crossing bar made t/r worse (+0.015 to +0.024).
- Release notes on GitHub now stop at the previous pre-release.

1.0.0-beta.1 (2026-10-18): the 1.0 candidate; the final release follows one
more reading-test session
- t/r and t/1 join the measured pairs, so the regression gate guards them.
  A reader found t and r hard to tell apart (t/r 0.936 at 12 px; Radon
  0.790). Nineteen redrawn t's were measured; each one that separated t
  from r pushed it toward 1, f or l (a left-reaching bar: t/1 +0.10 at
  20 px, "texto" read as "1ex1o"). The beta keeps the 0.8.0 t; the fix is
  t and r (or t and 1) redrawn together, in a later beta.
- fontbakery's universal profile passes (0 FAIL, 0 ERROR): the font
  revision agrees with the name table, Windows clipping metrics are family
  constants that cover every glyph, a STAT table, the italic caret slope,
  no legacy Mac name records.
- A reading-speed self-test page: blind, counterbalanced passages in Jandi
  Mono, Wide, Text and Radon, with fresh passages every session, a
  practice passage, pace checks and four-option comprehension questions.
  So far no font is measurably faster for its reader; Jandi Mono stays the
  default.
- Reading-size guidance in the README; a palette-bridge line on the
  specimen (azulejo cobalt, Ataíde's red, talha gold).
- Faster builds with byte-identical output (one font: 104 s -> 50 s), and
  CI in parallel jobs: five builds, the regression gate, and fontbakery QA
  per family; releases wait for both checks.
- The README opens with a new image (docs/hero.png, made by tools/hero.sh)
  in place of the Renaissance-era one. Code comments are all in English, and
  CLAUDE.md sets the code standard for the refactor that follows the beta.

0.8.0 (2026-10-17): ornament, all opt-in (tools/ornament.py)
- Florões: the hedera at ❦ ❧ ☙; rocalha leaves, vine leaves and buds at
  U+1F650-1F667, with open scrolled stems.
- Cartelas after Manuel da Costa Ataíde's ceiling cartouches at ❨❩ ❪❫ ❲❳ ❴❵.
- Ribbon ends after the Congonhas prophets' scrolls at ❰ ❱ (Unicode has no
  banner code points). ❮ ❯ keep Radon's design (❯ is Starship's prompt).
- Capitulares: titl (carved inline capitals) and swsh (Figueiredo's
  flourished capitals), OpenType features off by default.
- Default text is identical to 0.7.0: every measured number is unchanged.
- build.sh caps the autohinter at two workers per font (memory).

0.7.0 (2026-10-17): outline quality and the Text weight
- Curves: the pen's polygon outlines are fitted back into cubic Béziers
  (tools/fitcurves.py, Schneider 1990), corners kept sharp, within about
  1 unit of the pen shape. Letters average 26 segments instead of 55
  (Radon 18).
- Hinting with AFDKO's otfautohint, with alignment zones measured on each
  style's ink. i l j q 1 (and t f in the italics) stay unhinted, with their
  families: hinted, they made narrow pairs more alike. Sharpness at 12 px
  0.136 -> 0.157 (Regular), 0.127 -> 0.165 (Italic); Radon 0.158.
- m and w narrowed (stems 300/600/900): mm mw ww were the tightest pairs.
- Ball terminals are now the punctuation's drop (gota). The dots of i j
  and the diacritics stay dabs: as drops they made i/l worse.
- Jandi Mono Text and Text NF: Regular and Italic with the nib 8% wider,
  weight 450; stems +8%, thinnest stroke 0.98 px at 12 px.
- Measured against 0.6.0: no confusable pair worse in Regular, Italic or
  Wide; spacing CV 0.239 Regular, 0.256 Italic, 0.196 Wide (Radon 0.267).
- New specimen (docs/specimen.png, tools/specimen.sh) with Figueiredo's
  plate nº 7; build.sh runs at most JOBS processes at once (default 4).

0.6.0 (2026-10-17): pen punctuation and ligatures
- Punctuation drawn with the pen (tools/punct.py): . , : ; ' " ! ? ( ) [ ] { }.
  Their unit is the drop (gota). The comma is a smaller drop with a long
  tail. Braces are the carved C-scroll with a pointed middle, parentheses
  deep bows, brackets long-armed. Each sits on Radon's own centre.
- Code symbols (- _ / \ * + = < > | @ # & ~ ^) and every ligature glyph
  re-stroked: the nib is swept along the medial axis of Radon's outline, so
  Radon's geometry and cross-cell joins stay but the weight is the pen's.
  Dots inside ligatures become drops; the solid |> triangle stays solid.
  All are sheared to Jandí's 7° / 13°.
- Ligatures on by default: Radon's ss01-ss10 moved into liga (== != -> =>
  <= >= |> :: ... ### and the rest). -liga turns them all off; texture
  healing (calt) is untouched.
- Measured against 0.5.0: no confusable pair worse in Regular, Italic or
  Wide; ,/. :/; (/[ [/{ at or below Radon's at 12 px.

0.5.0 (2026-10-17): spacing
- Jandi Mono Wide and Wide NF: the same letters in a 1320 cell (extra-large
  letter spacing helped dyslexic readers in Zorzi et al. 2012). Only glyphs
  that touch both cell edges (box drawing, blocks) stretch; brackets and
  punctuation keep their width and are centred.
- Optical centring for r and k (centre of mass, not bounding box).
- Texture healing re-derived from Jandí's own widths: m and w no longer grow
  beside narrow letters; narrow letters keep Radon's step aside.
- Spacing variation (CV of bigram ink gaps) 0.283 -> 0.253 Regular,
  0.302 -> 0.271 Italic, 0.208 in Wide; Radon is 0.267.
- Capital T and Z widened to nearly the full cell, as in Radon. Their short
  bars left a hole between neighbours (TT read as "T T", most visible in
  the launcher): the gap at bar height went from 13.2 to 10.5 px at 40 px.
- Mark-to-mark positioning (mkmk) and mark anchors on precomposed accented
  letters: stacked accents such as ǘ ǚ now sit one above the other.
- No confusable pair worse than 0.4.0 by more than 0.01 (docs/metrics.md),
  except v/y +0.011 at 12 px in Wide, a pixel-phase effect of the wider cell.

0.4.0 (2026-10-17): Barroco na letra
- The base inspiration is now the Luso-Brazilian Baroque and the rococó
  mineiro (see ROADMAP.md). The letters follow Manuel de Andrade de
  Figueiredo, Nova Escola para aprender a ler, escrever e contar (Lisbon,
  1722; public domain, Biblioteca Nacional de Portugal, purl.pt/107).
- Regular and Bold, after plate nº 9: larger hooked ascenders on b d h k,
  closed by a ball; ball terminals (the pen's drop) on c f j r y, C G J S and
  the figures 2 3 5 6 9; lapidary foot serifs on F H K M N P R T.
- Italic and Bold Italic become a corsiva after plate nº 7: looped h k, a
  loop on l kept above the x-height, the bastarda d, a rasgo on p, long
  lead-ins and exits, and n and u that differ at both ends.
- Measured against 0.3.0 (docs/metrics.md): no confusable pair worse by more
  than 0.01 in either style at any size. The italic's mean similarity fell
  0.894 → 0.884 at 12 px, and roman/italic similarity 0.976 → 0.968.
- Tried and dropped: Figueiredo's pressure quill. It filled the counters at
  terminal sizes, so both italics keep the broad nib.

0.3.0 (2026-10-16)
- Measurement harness (tools/measure.py): confusability of easily confused
  characters, spacing evenness, roman/italic similarity, visual angle of the
  x-height, sharpness. Numbers in docs/metrics.md (0.2.0 baseline kept).
- x-height raised 5% (1027 → 1080 units): 0.205° → 0.216° at 12 pt on a
  97 PPI screen at 60 cm, above the ≈0.2° critical print size of reading
  research (Legge & Bigelow 2011). Ascenders keep their height.
- Distinctiveness pass on the most-confused letters (Beier & Larson 2010):
  mean similarity 0.908 → 0.894 at 12 px, no pair worse by > 0.01. New forms:
  narrower notched a, wider o, footed b, long-exit h, short lower hook on f
  vs a big round hook on j, wider i, a sweeping tail on l, a t with a
  right-reaching bar, wider I bars, a longer flag on 1.

0.2.0 (2026-10-16)
- Cell narrowed from 1240 to 1160 units (0.62 → 0.58 em): with the narrower
  chancery letters, 1240 left too much space between them. Every glyph kept
  from Radon (symbols, box drawing, blocks, powerline) and every NF icon is
  scaled horizontally to the new cell, so box lines still join.
- Docs: braille comes only with the NF builds; upstream Radon has none.

0.1.0 (2026-10-16)
- Redrawn from centreline skeletons swept by a rectangular nib at 35°, slant
  7° (Regular/Bold) and 13° (Italic/Bold Italic): a–z, A–Z, 0–9, dotless i/j,
  ß æ ø œ ł đ ħ ĸ ŋ þ ð Æ Ø Œ Ł Đ Ð Ħ Ŋ Þ, every precomposed accented Latin
  letter in Radon's character set, 17 combining marks and their .case and
  Vietnamese stacked forms.
- Added GPOS mark attachment and corrected the GDEF class of the combining
  marks (Radon has no GPOS and files its marks as components), so sequences
  such as g + U+0303 (Guarani g̃) and G + U+0303 position correctly.
- Regenerated Radon's texture-healing variants (.left/.right/.both) from the
  same skeletons, so its contextual alternates keep working.
- Distinct confusables: flagged and footed 1, curved l, serifed I, slashed 0.
- Czech/Slovak carons on ď ľ ť Ľ drawn as apostrophes.
- NF builds: 10,828 Nerd Fonts icon glyphs copied from MonaspiceRn Nerd Font
  (Nerd Fonts' patch of Radon, already scaled to this cell).

Still from Radon in 0.1.0: punctuation and symbols, zero.alt01–04, i.alt and
l.alt, A.italic/B.italic, numerator/denominator/superior/inferior figures.
